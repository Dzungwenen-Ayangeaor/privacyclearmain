Our Mission

We believe informed consent should actually be informed.

PrivacyClear was built on a single conviction: the consent architecture of the internet is broken. We're fixing it — one policy at a time.

Why we exist

Every time someone downloads an app or creates an account, they face a legal contract written at post-graduate reading level, averaging 2,500–8,000 words. Research shows it would take 76 work days per year for Americans to read every policy they encounter. Almost no one does.

This is not accidental. Complexity is a legal shield. Companies bury data-sharing clauses, tracking permissions, and indefinite retention schedules across hundreds of lines of legalese — invisible to ordinary people.

Regulators like GDPR and CCPA mandate disclosure. They cannot mandate comprehension. That gap is where PrivacyClear lives.

We built an AI-powered transparency layer that makes every policy readable in under 30 seconds — regardless of your legal expertise.

01
Radical Transparency
We say what policies actually mean, not a sanitized version. If something is genuinely bad for users, we say so — clearly.
02
Accuracy Over Speed
We never hallucinate clauses. Our AI is instructed to flag uncertainty rather than guess. Trust is built on accuracy, not impressiveness.
03
Universal Access
Privacy transparency should not require a law degree, a subscription, or fluency in English. We're building for everyone.
04
Independence
We do not accept payment from companies to improve their scores. Our analysis is algorithmic and conflict-free.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PrivacyClear collects only what is required to operate the service: your account email, the policies you submit for analysis, and basic usage data needed for billing and abuse prevention. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share submitted policy text with third parties for advertising purposes.

Analysis results may be cached to speed up repeat lookups of the same publicly available policy. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time from account settings or by emailing privacy@privacyclear.io.
